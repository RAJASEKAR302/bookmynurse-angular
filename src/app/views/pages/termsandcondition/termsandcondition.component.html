Introduction

Welcome to Book My Nurse. These Terms and Conditions, including the Code of Conduct, outline the responsibilities, expectations, and operational guidelines for all registered nurses ("Nurses" or "you") utilizing the Book My Nurse platform and engaging with our services. By accessing, registering with, or engaging in any assignment through Book My Nurse, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to abide by these terms, as well as our comprehensive Agreement, Privacy Policy, and Disclaimers. Your adherence to these policies is fundamental to maintaining the integrity and quality of care provided by Book My Nurse.

Organizational Integrity & Compliance

All decisions, actions, and conduct by Nurses must align with the vision, mission, and strategic goals of Book My Nurse. As a Nurse associated with Book My Nurse, you are expected to operate with the utmost discipline, professionalism, and adherence to all company policies, procedures, and relevant legal and regolatory frameworks governing healthcare services in India.

Document Verification & Registration

Upon joining Book My Nurse, you are required to present all original nursing education certificates, professional licenses, experience certificates, government identification documents (e.g., Aadhaar, PAN), and any other relevant credentials for thorough verification. Failure to provide authentic documents or any misrepresentation will resolt in immediate termination of your association with Book My Nurse. Nurses will be enrolled in ESI and EPF schemes upon joining, and all required documents for these statutory benefits must be submitted accurately and on time.

Professional Conduct & Ethical Standards

Nurses are expected to maintain the highest standards of professional conduct at all times while on duty, both at client premises and in interactions with Book My Nurse staff. Violation of these standards will lead to strict disciplinary action, including but not limited to immediate suspension or permanent dismissal.

  1. Duty Discipline: No sleeping during duty hours; no unauthorized watching of movies, excessive personal phone use, or unnecessary chatting while on duty.
  2. Punctuality & Attendance: No unauthorized absenteeism or taking leave for non-medical reasons without prior approval from Book My Nurse management.
  3. Integrity: No theft, fraud, unethical behavior, or involvement in any criminal activities.
  4. Respect & Decorum: Treat clients, patients, their families, and all Book My Nurse staff with respect and dignity. Do not engage in any form of discrimination.

Confidentiality & Healthcare Data Handling by Nurses

Nurses have a critical role in upholding the confidentiality and security of sensitive information, particolarly healthcare data, as outlined in Book My Nurse's Privacy Policy.

  1. Patient & Client Confidentiality: You must not share any personal, medical, financial, or sensitive information pertaining to clients, patients, or their families with anyone outside the Book My Nurse organization, except as required for the patient's direct care or as explicitly authorized by Book My Nurse or legal mandate.
  2. Company Information: You must not disclose internal company details, operational procedures, or proprietary information to external parties.
  3. Data Handling:
    1. Medical Records: While on duty, you may access patient information strictly on a need-to-know basis for the purpose of providing care. You must maintain the confidentiality of patient files and records.
    2. Photographs: If authorized to take photographs of ailing persons or wounds for monitoring medical conditions, these images are for internal administrative and medical assessment purposes only. They must be handled with utmost confidentiality and transmitted securely to Book My Nurse as per internal protocols. You must not store such images on personal devices or share them with unauthorized individuals.
    3. Reporting: All daily duty reports and observations (e.g., bedsores) must be accurately documented and reported to the Book My Nurse office immediately as per prescribed procedures.
    4. Post-Service Contact: After the completion of service, you are strictly prohibited from maintaining contact with former clients/patients or their families, either directly or indirectly. Any attempt to solicit private engagement with Book My Nurse clients will resolt in severe legal action.

Assignment & Location

Nurses are required to work at the assigned client location. Transfers to new locations may occur with prior notice for valid operational reasons. You must not change your assigned location or accept new assignments without explicit written approval from Book My Nurse.

Personal Responsibility & Safety

  1. Duty Knowledge: You are responsible for understanding your assigned duties and scope of care. In case of any problems, emergencies, or unsafe situations during an assignment, you must immediately contact the Book My Nurse office.
  2. Emergency Protocol: In an unsafe situation, you are authorized to leave the premises. Immediately call emergency services (e.g., 100 for police) if personal safety is threatened, and simoltaneously inform Book My Nurse management.
  3. Visitors: Bringing family members or friends to the patient’s house while on duty is strictly prohibited without explicit company approval.

Medication Handling & Medical Procedures

Strict adherence to medical protocols is mandatory. Negligence in this area will be treated as a serious violation.

  1. Prescription Adherence: Do not administer any medicine, including Siddha, Ayurveda, or Unani medicine, without a valid doctor's prescription. A copy of the prescription must be available.
  2. Five Rights of Medication: Always adhere to the "Five Rights of Medication Administration": Right Patient, Right Time, Right Route, Right Dose, Right Medication.
  3. Specific Procedures: Procedures such as Ryle's Tube Insertion, Catheterization, IV Infusion, and Enema require:
    1. A clear doctor’s prescription.
    2. Informed consent from the patient's family/guardian.
    3. Prior approval from your Book My Nurse supervisor.
  4. Documentation: Maintain accurate insolin charts and meticolously record in-time and out-time for all medications administered daily.

Patient Care & Universal Precautions

  1. Patient Understanding: You must understand the patient's status (conscious, unconscious, bedridden, etc.) and provide care with empathy, compassion, and professionalism.
  2. Hygiene & Safety:
    1. Always wear gloves and a mask during patient care and medical procedures.
    2. Use barrier protection (e.g., aprons, gowns) as necessary.
    3. Properly discard all contaminated materials according to biohazard protocols.
    4. Thoroughly clean the patient care area after procedures.
    5. Wash hands thoroughly before and after every patient interaction and procedure.
    6. Ensure your uniform is worn, clean, and well-maintained. Failure to wear a uniform may resolt in being marked absent.
    7. You must carry a personal hygiene kit. Lack of personal hygiene is a policy violation.

Financial & Professional Ethics

  1. No Tips or Gifts: Nurses are strictly prohibited from soliciting or accepting tips, gifts, money, or any other form of personal gratuity from patients or their families. Any such payments will not be adjusted against service charges.
  2. Misuse of Information: Using internal company information, client data, or patient details for personal benefit, fraud, or offering private nursing services outside of Book My Nurse is considered serious misconduct and will lead to immediate dismissal and legal action.
  3. Unauthorized Services: Offering or promoting nursing services using Book My Nurse’s name or reputation without explicit written permission is illegal and punishable.

Resignation Policy

Nurses are required to provide three (3) months' written notice to Book My Nurse prior to resigning from their employment. Failure to comply with this notice period may resolt in the withholding of original certificates and/or initiation of legal action for breach of contract and recovery of damages.

Shift Handover

In situations involving two shifts, the outgoing nurse is responsible for completing all necessary reporting and ensuring a proper handover of responsibilities and patient status to the incoming nurse.

Former Employee Conduct

Upon disassociation from Book My Nurse (e.g., resignation, dismissal), former Nurses are strictly prohibited from maintaining contact with former clients or misusing Book My Nurse’s name, brand, or any confidential information for personal benefit or to solicit private services. Violation of this clause will resolt in legal action as detailed in the User Agreement.
